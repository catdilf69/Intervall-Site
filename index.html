A note from Ruben / Why I built it

I just wanted
an interval timer.

I was tired of downloading workout apps full of ads, or being asked to pay five dollars a month just to create an interval workout. Too often, the design was frustrating too.

So I built Intervall: something simple, considered, and enjoyable to use. A timer I actually wanted to train with.
